# Metal Hydride Hydrogen Compressor (MHHC)

A numerical simulation project investigating the thermodynamic behavior of a Metal Hydride Hydrogen Compressor (MHHC) using COMSOL Multiphysics.

---

## Project Overview

Metal Hydride Hydrogen Compressors use the reversible absorption and desorption of hydrogen within metal hydrides to compress hydrogen without moving mechanical components.

This project focuses on developing a numerical model to simulate hydrogen absorption and desorption in a LaNi₅-based metal hydride system and evaluating the effects of temperature and pressure on compressor performance.

---

## Objectives

- Study the working principle of Metal Hydride Hydrogen Compressors
- Develop a mathematical model for hydrogen absorption.
- Simulate coupled heat and mass transfer during charging and discharging
- Investigate the influence of temperature on compressor performance
- Validate simulation results against published literature

---

- COMSOL Multiphysics
- MATLAB (post-processing)

---

## Engineering Background

Metal hydrides absorb hydrogen at low temperatures and pressures while releasing heat.

When heated, the hydride releases hydrogen at a higher pressure, allowing hydrogen compression without conventional mechanical compressors.

The numerical model considers:

- Hydrogen absorption/desorption kinetics
- Heat transfer
- Mass transfer
- Thermodynamic equilibrium
- Pressure evolution

## Results

The developed model successfully simulated:

- Hydrogen absorption
- Temperature evolution


Simulation results showed good agreement with published literature (approximately 97% agreement).
